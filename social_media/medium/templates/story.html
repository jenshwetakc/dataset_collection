A useful practice begins with observation. Instead of reaching immediately for a framework, spend time with the details: the point where a task becomes confusing, the question a colleague hesitates to ask, the small workaround that everyone quietly accepts.

Write down what happened before deciding what it means. Try one small change, then return to the same situation and look again. This makes learning less abstract and gives other people something concrete to respond to.
