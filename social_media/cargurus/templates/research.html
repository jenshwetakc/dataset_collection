Choosing the right compact SUV

By CarGurus Research • Synthetic article

Start by considering how you use your vehicle: daily commuting, weekend trips, passenger space, or cargo needs.

Compare the features you will use

Look at seating, visibility, cabin layout, storage, and the availability of driver-assistance features. Compare specific trim levels rather than relying only on a model name.

Plan a test drive

Spend time checking the seating position, controls, road noise, and cargo area. Ask the seller about maintenance records and vehicle history.
